About Rooststead

Is Rooststead a veterinarian?

No. Rooststead is animal-care reference information. It does not diagnose or treat your animal, and its answers are written by an AI system that can be wrong. Confirm every dose and drug with the product label and a veterinarian. Prescription (Rx) items need a veterinarian’s prescription. Rooststead is for animals only, never for people. The Terms of Use say this in full.

What is a lookup?

A lookup is a question that Rooststead’s server answers for you: a diagnostic, a care protocol, a research question, the medication reference, a medication lookup, the grass guide, a grass lookup or a Field Computer question it cannot answer on your device. The server passes it to Anthropic’s Claude, an AI system, which writes the answer. Reference photos come with a lookup and do not count as one.

What is the Field Computer?

Tap the round Computer button and ask in plain words. What it can work out from your own records it answers on your device at once, with no connection and without using a lookup: which lumps grew, your animals and library, unit conversions and sums, due dates and hatch dates, and how the app works. Words that sound like an emergency get “call your veterinarian or an emergency clinic now” straight away. Any other question goes out as a lookup, after you agree, with the chosen animal’s details and its lump log. Each answer is marked From this device or Lookup. It never works out a dose on the device.

Does it need the internet?

Lookups do. Your animals on file, the lump log, the Tow-it-in list, the library, backup and the Field Computer’s answers from your own records work with no connection.

Where is the lump log?

Open the Animals tab and go to the Lump log section.

Free lookups and Rooststead Pro

What is free, and what does Rooststead Pro add?

Rooststead is a free download. Animals on file, the lump log, the Tow-it-in list, the library, backup and restore, and settings are always free. Each install also gets 3 free lookups in total. They do not come back once they are used. Rooststead Pro gives up to 50 lookups a day. It costs $19.99 a month or $149.99 a year in the United States; the price in your country is shown in the app before you buy. There is no free trial.

How do I subscribe?

When your free lookups are used and you ask for another one, the app opens the plans screen. Pick Pro Monthly or Pro Yearly and confirm with Apple. Payment goes through your Apple Account. The subscription renews automatically until you cancel it.

I have Rooststead Pro. How do I get it on another iPhone or iPad, or after installing the app again?

Sign in with the same Apple Account, then tap Restore Purchases. It is on the plans screen and in the app’s Settings.

How do I cancel Rooststead Pro?

On your iPhone or iPad, open the device’s Settings › your name › Subscriptions › Rooststead, and cancel there. Cancel at least 24 hours before the renewal date. Rooststead Pro stays on until the end of the period you paid for. Deleting the app does not cancel a subscription.

How do I get a refund?

Apple takes the payment, so refunds are Apple’s to grant. Ask at reportaproblem.apple.com. We cannot refund an Apple purchase ourselves.

It says I have used today’s lookups.

Rooststead Pro has a fair-use cap of 50 lookups a day for each subscription, on all your devices together. It starts again at midnight UTC.

A lookup failed. Did it count?

No. A lookup is counted only when an answer is delivered. If the app says the service is busy or the lookup did not finish, wait a minute and try again.

Lookups and photos

My photo will not attach.

Try these in order:

  • A diagnostic lookup takes up to three photos. Remove one before you add another.
  • If the camera does not open, open your device’s Settings, find Rooststead and turn on Camera.
  • Try a different photo, or take a screenshot of the photo and attach the screenshot.
  • If it still fails, email us and say what the app showed.

The answer looks wrong.

It can be. Answers are written by an AI system from what you entered, and they can be wrong or incomplete. Do not act on an answer that looks wrong: check with a veterinarian. Then please tell us. Email the kind of lookup, what you entered and what looked wrong, and we will look into it.

Why is there no photo beside an item?

Reference photos come from Wikimedia Commons and are shown only when a fitting one is found. Some items have none. A reference photo is an example. It may not show what your animal has.

Your records and your privacy

Where are my records saved?

On your device, inside the app. There is no account and nothing syncs. We cannot see them.

How do I move my records to a new phone?

Use Backup and restore in the app. Make a backup on the old phone and keep it somewhere you can reach from the new one. On the new phone, install Rooststead, open Backup and restore, and load the backup. If you have Rooststead Pro, also tap Restore Purchases on the new phone.

How do I erase everything?

Open Settings in the app and tap Erase everything on this device. This removes your animals, the lump log, the library and the rest of your saved records from the device. It cannot be undone, so make a backup first if you may want them back. It does not cancel a subscription.

What does Rooststead count, and can I turn it off?

To make the answers better, our server keeps counts of what is looked up: the kind of lookup, the species, the subject in a few words (such as “ear yeast” or “terbinafine”) and the country or US state the request came from. It also keeps a copy of each answered lookup, its photos and its answer, with no set end date, with emails, phone numbers, street addresses and the place stored inside photos removed, and none of it is tied to you. To turn this off, open Settings, tap Manage under Privacy & data, and tap Disable under Usage analytics. The Privacy Policy has the details.

What is “Did this help?”

It is how you tell us whether an answer was useful. Tap Yes or No under an answer. If you tap No, you can say why, add a short note, and tick a box to send us the question and the answer so we can check it. Nothing is sent unless you tap, and photos are never sent with it. We read what comes in and use it to correct the answers.

How do I withdraw the AI permission?

Before your first lookup, the app shows what will be sent and asks you to agree. To take that agreement back, open Settings in the app, tap Manage under Privacy & data, and tap Revoke under Data processing consent. The app then sends no lookups until you agree again. Your records keep working. The Privacy Policy says what a lookup sends and to whom.
