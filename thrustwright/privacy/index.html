Privacy Policy

Effective November 22, 2026

ThrustWright is a jet engine sizing app for iPhone and iPad made by Angel Tech Corporation Industries L.L.C. (“we”, “us”). This policy explains what happens to your information when you use it.

The short version
  • ThrustWright collects no data. We don’t receive anything about you or how you use the app.
  • No accounts, no ads, no tracking, no analytics.
  • ThrustWright Pro, the optional subscription, is bought through Apple: we never see your payment details or your Apple Account.
  • The app works offline and makes no internet connections of its own (buying or restoring Pro goes through Apple’s App Store).
  • Your designs and settings stay on your device.

What stays on your device

The app saves the design you are working on, the designs you choose to keep (up to 40) and your choice of millimetres or inches. This is stored only on your device, inside the app. We can’t see it, and it isn’t sent anywhere.

To erase a saved design, tap Designs at the top of the app and delete it. Deleting the app deletes everything it saved.

Files you save

When you save patterns, a drawing, a picture or a design sheet, the app hands the file to your device’s share sheet. It goes only where you send it: for example the Files app, AirDrop, Mail or a printer. We never receive it.

ThrustWright Pro purchases

ThrustWright Pro is sold through Apple’s App Store. Apple handles the payment and your Apple Account under Apple’s privacy policy; we never receive your name, payment details or Apple Account. To know whether Pro is active, the app asks Apple on your device and keeps a small note there (which plan, and when it renews). Apple gives us only sales and subscription reports that don’t identify you.

Network connections

Everything ThrustWright needs (the engine model, drawings and fonts) is inside the app, so it works in airplane mode and contacts no servers of ours. The only connections are to Apple’s App Store when you look at, buy, restore or manage ThrustWright Pro. The only way to leave the app is a link you choose to tap, such as this policy or the support page. Those open in your web browser, where the website’s own privacy terms apply. Our pages are hosted by GitHub Pages; like most web hosts, GitHub may keep short technical logs, such as IP addresses, to run and secure the service. We don’t use them to identify anyone.

Children

ThrustWright collects no personal information from anyone, so it collects none from children. Building and running engines is not an activity for children: see the Terms of Use.

Your rights

We hold no data about you, so there is nothing for us to access, correct or delete. If you have questions about privacy, email us and we’ll reply within 30 days.

Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we’ll update this page and the date at the top. If a future version of the app ever collected any data, we would say so here and in the release notes before that version came out.
