First-cut sizing, not a certified design

ThrustWright estimates the size and performance of small model and experimental jet engines from simplified engineering models. It is meant to put a design in the right range before you build and test it. No engineer, regulator or other authority has reviewed or approved any design it produces. Its results, drawings, flat patterns, drill tables and limit checks can contain errors, and a design that passes every check in the app can still fail.

Before you cut metal or run an engine, check every dimension, material and limit yourself, or have a qualified engineer check them. The accuracy we have measured is described in the app on the Model check tab; treat every number as an estimate.

Jet engines are dangerous

Building, testing and running a jet engine involves parts turning at very high speed, fire, hot gas, fuel under pressure and extreme noise. A wheel that bursts throws fragments with roughly the energy of a rifle bullet. These hazards can cause serious injury, death and damage to property.

  • Only build and run an engine if you have the skill, tools and protective equipment to do it safely, and follow the test-stand rules on the app’s Safety screen.
  • Follow the laws and rules where you live, including noise, fire, burn-ban and fuel-storage rules.
  • Keep children and bystanders away from any engine and its test area.
  • Do not use ThrustWright to design any engine that carries people, or for any certified, commercial or safety-critical use.
