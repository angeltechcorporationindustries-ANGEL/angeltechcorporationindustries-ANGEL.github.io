Privacy Policy

Effective November 19, 2026

Combo Buster is a game made by Angel Tech Corporation Industries L.L.C. (“we”, “us”). This policy explains what the app collects, why, and the choices you have.

The short version
  • No accounts, no ads, no tracking, no analytics.
  • Your game progress stays on your device.
  • If the global leaderboard is on, the app sends an anonymous player ID, your board name (picked at random from a word list), your scores and level, and whether you own the VIP Pass. Switch the leaderboard off in Settings to delete that data.

Data that stays on your device

Coins, levels, unlocked and equipped items, settings, daily missions, your login streak and your best scores are saved on your device. We can’t see them. Deleting the app deletes them.

Global leaderboard

The leaderboard is on by default so you can see how you rank. While it’s on, the app sends this to our leaderboard service:

  • A random player ID created on your device. It isn’t connected to your name, email address, Apple Account, phone number or any device identifier.
  • A random secret that proves an entry is yours, so only your device can update or delete it. We store only a one-way hash of it.
  • Your board name. The app picks it from a fixed list of family-friendly words, such as “Lucky Comet 42”. You can’t type your own, so it never contains personal information.
  • Your scores and level: your best score overall and for the current day, your level, and whether you own the VIP Pass (shown as a crown).

Other players can see your board name, scores, level and VIP crown. We use this data only to run the leaderboard. We don’t sell it, share it for advertising, or combine it with other data.

Where it’s stored

The leaderboard runs on Supabase, a database service that stores the data for us on servers in the United States. Like most online services, Supabase may keep short-lived technical logs, such as IP addresses, to operate and secure the service. We don’t use these logs to identify players.

How long we keep it

Until you switch the leaderboard off or ask us to delete it. Daily rankings start fresh every day at midnight UTC; all-time bests stay until deleted.

Switching the leaderboard off and deleting your data

In the app, open Settings and switch off Global leaderboard. The app immediately asks the server to delete your entry and stops sending scores. If you switch it back on later, you join again with a new anonymous ID.

If you can’t use the app (for example, you deleted it), email us your board name, your approximate best score and the date you last played, and we’ll delete the matching entry.

Purchases

In-app purchases are processed by Apple. We never receive your payment details or your Apple Account information. Apple tells the app which items you own so it can unlock them and restore them on your devices.

Sharing your score

Tapping Share after a run opens the iOS share sheet with a short message about your score. Nothing is sent unless you choose an app to share with.

Children

Combo Buster is suitable for all ages. We don’t knowingly collect personal information from children. The leaderboard uses only an anonymous ID and a randomly picked name, and anyone can switch it off in Settings.

Your choices and rights

You can switch the leaderboard off at any time. Depending on where you live (for example the EU, the UK or California), you may have the right to access, correct or delete your data. Email us and we’ll reply within 30 days.

Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we’ll update this page and the date at the top. If a change affects what the app collects, we’ll also say so in the app’s release notes.
