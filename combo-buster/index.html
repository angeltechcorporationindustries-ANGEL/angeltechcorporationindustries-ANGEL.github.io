Combo Buster

Tap the moment the comet meets the star.

For iPhone and iPad.

Combo Buster is a one-tap timing game that’s easy to learn and hard to put down. A comet races around a glowing ring: tap the moment it meets the star. Every hit speeds it up, flips its direction and tightens the window. One miss ends the run.

  • Chain perfects into combos. Three perfects in a row raise your multiplier, all the way to ×5.
  • Make it yours. Unlock comets, trails, themes, hit effects and sound packs.
  • Play every day. Daily gifts, a login streak and three fresh missions each day.
  • Climb the global board under an anonymous name, or switch it off and play privately.
  • No ads. Everything for sale is cosmetic or coins, so the board stays fair.
