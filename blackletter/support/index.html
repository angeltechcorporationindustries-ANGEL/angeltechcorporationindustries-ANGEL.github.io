Questions

How do I start?

Open the app and agree to the Terms of Use. Today shows the day’s checklist. Practice writes AI practice questions, Rules holds the built-in rules, Writing has essays, short-answer sets and performance tasks, and the Shop sells Blackletter Pro. Choose NextGen or the legacy UBE, and your exam date, in Settings (the button at the top right).

What is free?

Always free: the 144 built-in rules with spaced review, your own rules, your missed questions, scores, timers, the daily checklist and settings. To try the AI features, each install gets, in total: 10 AI practice questions, 1 written task, 1 grading, 3 follow-up answers and 1 set of rule cards.

How do I get Blackletter Pro?

Open the Shop tab, choose a plan and tap Subscribe. Apple charges your Apple Account and the plan renews automatically until you cancel. There is no free trial.

How do I cancel?

On your iPhone or iPad open Settings › your name › Subscriptions › Blackletter and tap Cancel Subscription, or in the app tap Shop › Manage subscription. Blackletter Pro stays on until the end of the period you paid for. Deleting the app does not cancel a subscription.

How do I get a refund?

Apple takes the payment, so refunds are Apple’s to grant: ask at reportaproblem.apple.com. We cannot refund an Apple purchase ourselves.

I subscribed on another device, or reinstalled the app

Sign in with the same Apple Account and tap Shop › Restore purchases.

A question or a grade looks wrong

AI can be wrong, even after the second check. Tap Ask about this question to have it explained, or Throw out this question to take it out of your scores and your missed list. Check the rule against your bar review course. If a built-in rule looks wrong, email us its name and why.

It says my free tries or today’s allowance are used

Free tries are counted for each install, in total, and do not come back. Blackletter Pro’s daily allowances start again at midnight UTC (6 p.m. Central Standard Time, 7 p.m. during daylight saving time).

It says the AI is busy or unavailable

Try again in a minute. A request that fails is not counted.

How do I stop sending anything to AI?

In the app open Settings and tap Withdraw my agreement. Nothing is sent after that, and the app asks again before the next AI request. Everything that does not use AI keeps working.

Where is my data? Can you restore it?

Your study records are saved only on your device, and deleting the app erases them. There is no account, so we cannot see or restore them.

Does it work offline?

The built-in rules, spaced review, your own rules, your missed questions, timers and scores all work with no connection. The AI features need one.

Is this legal advice?

No. Blackletter is a study aid. Section 3 of the Terms of Use explains what that means.

How do I opt out of arbitration?

Email angeltechcorporationindustries@gmail.com within 30 days after you first tap I agree (Settings shows the date), with the subject “Arbitration opt-out”, your name, that date and a clear statement that you do not want to arbitrate. Section 17.9 of the Terms has the details.

I found a security problem

Please email us before telling anyone else. Section 10 of the Terms describes the good-faith security research rule.
