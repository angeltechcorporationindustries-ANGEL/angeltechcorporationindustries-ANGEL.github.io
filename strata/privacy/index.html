Privacy Policy

Effective November 23, 2026

The short version
  • Strata collects no personal information and sends nothing to us or anyone else.
  • No account, no ads, no analytics, no tracking.
  • What you write or save in the app stays on your device.

This policy covers the Strata app for iPhone and iPad, made by Angel Tech Corporation Industries L.L.C. (“we”, “us”).

What stays on your device

Strata works offline. These are kept only in the app’s storage on your device, never sent anywhere, and deleted when you delete the app:

  • Your field notebook entries, including any location you add to them.
  • Your field kit checklist, your best quiz score and the tab you were on.
  • Whether you have Strata Pro, as Apple last reported it.

Location

Strata asks for your location only when you tap Get GPS fix in the field notebook, and only to write it into that note on your device. It is never sent to us or anyone else. You can say no, or turn it off in Settings, and the notebook still works.

Motion sensors

The clinometer and compass read your device’s motion and orientation sensors only while that screen is running, to show dip and bearing. The readings are not stored or sent anywhere.

Purchases

Strata Pro is sold through Apple’s in-app purchase. Apple handles the payment and your Apple Account under Apple’s Privacy Policy; we receive no payment details. The app asks Apple on your device whether a subscription is active.

Children

Strata is suitable for all ages and collects no information from anyone, including children under 13.

Links

The Support, Privacy and Terms links open these pages in Safari. This website is hosted by GitHub Pages, which may keep standard server logs under GitHub’s privacy statement; we don’t add analytics or cookies.

Changes

If this policy changes, we’ll update this page and the date at the top. If the app ever needed to collect something, we would ask you in the app first.
