Strata™

Field geology reference for iPhone and iPad

Strata helps you identify a mineral or a rock with the tests you can do in your hand, look up its properties, and carry the charts and calculators of the field season in your pocket. It works offline, needs no account and collects nothing.

  • Identify: a mineral filter built on luster, hardness, streak, cleavage, color and special properties, and a step-by-step rock key.
  • 92 minerals and 73 rocks, each with how to recognize it, where it occurs, its look-alikes and its uses.
  • Geologic time: the whole time scale with its ages and key events, and an age lookup.
  • Reference: field tests, the Mohs scale, grain sizes, Bowen’s series, rock classification, metamorphic facies, the rock cycle, plate tectonics, structural geology, radiometric dating and a glossary.
  • Strata Pro (optional subscription): the strike and dip solver, a clinometer and compass from the phone’s sensors, a GPS field notebook and a quiz.
