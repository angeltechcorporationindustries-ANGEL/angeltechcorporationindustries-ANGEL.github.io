Questions people ask

How do I identify a mineral?

Open Identify and set what you can test: the luster, what scratches it, the streak on unglazed porcelain, the cleavage, its colors and anything special (fizzing in acid, magnetism, taste). The list narrows as you go. Test a fresh surface: weathered surfaces are softer and duller. More › Field tests shows each test step by step.

How do I identify a rock?

Open Identify, tap Rock, and answer the questions about its texture, grain size and minerals. Strata shows the likely rocks and how to tell them apart.

The compass or clinometer doesn’t move

Tap Start sensors and allow motion and orientation access when asked. If you said no, allow it again under Settings for the app. Keep the phone away from steel (a hammer, a vehicle, a magnet in the case): it throws the compass off. Phone sensors are good to about 5°; check anything important with a real compass-clinometer.

Where are my notebook entries kept?

Only on your device, inside the app. Use Copy all as text to keep a copy elsewhere: deleting the app deletes its notes.

How do I restore Strata Pro on a new device?

Open More › Shop › Restore purchases, signed in with the same Apple Account you subscribed with.

How do I cancel Strata Pro?

On your iPhone or iPad: Settings › your name › Subscriptions › Strata Pro, or More › Shop › Manage subscription in the app. Cancel at least 24 hours before the end of the current period to stop the next renewal. Refunds are handled by Apple at reportaproblem.apple.com.
