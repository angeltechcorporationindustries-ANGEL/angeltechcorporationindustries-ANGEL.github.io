Questions and answers

Is Khalohfeh free?

It is free to download, with no ads and no account to make. The periodic table, element profiles, the reference library, the helper and a sample of every section are free. Khalohfeh Pro ($19.99 a month or $149.99 a year in the United States, no free trial) unlocks every calculator, lesson, lab experiment, flashcard deck, problem set and 3D project. Items that need it are marked PRO.

I bought Khalohfeh Pro. How do I get it on another iPhone or iPad?

Sign in with the same Apple Account, then open Settings (the sliders button at the top right) › Khalohfeh Pro › Restore purchases.

How do I cancel Khalohfeh Pro?

In the app: Settings › Khalohfeh Pro › Manage subscription. Or on your iPhone or iPad: Settings › your name › Subscriptions › Khalohfeh. Cancel at least 24 hours before the renewal date; Pro stays open until the end of the period you paid for. Refunds are handled by Apple at reportaproblem.apple.com.

Does it need the internet?

No. Everything is inside the app, so it works offline and in airplane mode.

How does the helper work without the internet?

It answers from the app’s own lessons, reference pages, element and compound data, and runs the app’s calculators for you, all on your device. Tap the helper button at the top of any screen; it starts with tips for the page you’re on.

Where is my progress saved?

On your device, inside the app. It doesn’t move between devices, and deleting the app deletes it.

How do I start over?

Open Settings (the sliders button at the top right) › Your progress › Reset progress. Your settings are kept.

How do I switch between light and dark, or change the temperature unit?

Settings › Appearance (System, Light or Dark) and Temperatures in (kelvin, °C or °F).

A value differs from my textbook. Which is right?

Khalohfeh uses IUPAC atomic weights, CODATA constants and the CRC Handbook. Books round differently or use older values, so small differences are normal; where a common alternative exists, the app notes it beside the value. If a number looks clearly wrong, please tell us.

Can I do the lab experiments at home?

The virtual lab is a simulation for learning. Real chemicals can burn, poison or explode: only do real experiments with proper training and supervision, the right protective gear, and the safety data sheet for every chemical.

Which devices does it run on?

iPhone and iPad with iOS 15 or later.
