Privacy Policy

Effective November 21, 2026

Khalohfeh is a chemistry app for iPhone and iPad made by ANGEL TECH CORPORATION INDUSTRIES L.L.C. (“we”, “us”). This policy explains what happens to your information when you use it.

The short version
  • Khalohfeh collects no data. We don’t receive anything about you or how you use the app.
  • No accounts, no ads, no tracking, no analytics.
  • Khalohfeh Pro, the optional subscription, is bought through Apple: we never see your payment details or your Apple Account.
  • The app works offline and makes no internet connections of its own (buying or restoring Pro goes through Apple’s App Store).
  • Your progress and settings stay on your device.

What stays on your device

To remember where you are, the app saves your settings (theme and temperature unit), flashcard progress, problem-set scores, finished lessons, your lab notebook, the molecule on your 3D bench and the pages you opened recently. This is stored only on your device, inside the app. We can’t see it, and it isn’t sent anywhere.

To erase it, open Settings › Your progress › Reset progress in the app. Deleting the app deletes everything it saved.

The built-in helper

The helper answers from the lessons, data and calculators inside the app, on your device. What you type into it is not sent anywhere and is not saved.

Khalohfeh Pro purchases

Khalohfeh Pro is sold through Apple’s App Store. Apple handles the payment and your Apple Account under Apple’s privacy policy; we never receive your name, payment details or Apple Account. To know whether Pro is active, the app asks Apple on your device and keeps a small note there (which plan, and when it renews). Apple gives us only sales and subscription reports that don’t identify you.

Network connections

Everything Khalohfeh needs (lessons, data, pictures and fonts) is inside the app, so it works in airplane mode and contacts no servers of ours. The only connections are to Apple’s App Store when you look at, buy, restore or manage Khalohfeh Pro. The only way to leave the app is a link you choose to tap, such as this policy or the support page. Those open in your web browser, where the website’s own privacy terms apply. Our pages are hosted by GitHub Pages; like most web hosts, GitHub may keep short technical logs, such as IP addresses, to run and secure the service. We don’t use them to identify anyone.

Children

Khalohfeh is suitable for students of all ages. Because it collects no personal information from anyone, it collects none from children.

Your rights

We hold no data about you, so there is nothing for us to access, correct or delete. If you have questions about privacy, email us and we’ll reply within 30 days.

Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we’ll update this page and the date at the top. If a future version of the app ever collected any data, we would say so here and in the release notes before that version came out.
