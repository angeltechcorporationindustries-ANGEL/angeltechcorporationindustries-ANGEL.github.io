Privacy Policy

Effective November 20, 2026

Triple Point is a chemistry app for iPhone and iPad made by ANGEL TECH CORPORATION INDUSTRIES L.L.C. (“we”, “us”). This policy explains what happens to your information when you use it.

The short version
  • Triple Point collects no data. We don’t receive anything about you or how you use the app.
  • No accounts, no ads, no tracking, no analytics, no in-app purchases.
  • The app works offline and makes no internet connections of its own.
  • Your progress and settings stay on your device.

What stays on your device

To remember where you are, the app saves your settings (theme and temperature unit), flashcard progress, problem-set scores, finished lessons, your lab notebook, the molecule on your 3D bench and the pages you opened recently. This is stored only on your device, inside the app. We can’t see it, and it isn’t sent anywhere.

To erase it, open Settings › Your progress › Reset progress in the app. Deleting the app deletes everything it saved.

The built-in helper

The helper answers from the lessons, data and calculators inside the app, on your device. What you type into it is not sent anywhere and is not saved.

No network connections

Everything Triple Point needs (lessons, data, pictures and fonts) is inside the app, so it works in airplane mode and contacts no servers. The only way to leave the app is a link you choose to tap, such as this policy or the support page. Those open in your web browser, where the website’s own privacy terms apply. Our pages are hosted by GitHub Pages; like most web hosts, GitHub may keep short technical logs, such as IP addresses, to run and secure the service. We don’t use them to identify anyone.

Children

Triple Point is suitable for students of all ages. Because it collects no personal information from anyone, it collects none from children.

Your rights

We hold no data about you, so there is nothing for us to access, correct or delete. If you have questions about privacy, email us and we’ll reply within 30 days.

Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we’ll update this page and the date at the top. If a future version of the app ever collected any data, we would say so here and in the release notes before that version came out.
