Moose Simulator™

Live a year as a bull moose, on iPhone and iPad

by ANGEL TECH™

A whole year in a northern valley

  • Live like a moose: browse willow and birch, wade out for pond lilies, dive for pondweed, swim out to the island and bed down to chew your cud.
  • Keep yourself going: forage, water, sodium, heat and stamina, with a mineral lick to find and a lake to cool off in.
  • The wild pushes back: wolves test you, a cow defends her calf, and in the autumn rut a rival bull wants your ground.
  • Seasons and weather: a full day and night, rain, snow and morning mist, new antlers every summer and the aurora in winter.
  • A field journal of goals, each with a true fact about moose.
  • The Salt Lick: earn salt as you play and dress your bull in rare coats, from chestnut to the white spirit moose, and antler finishes up to a golden rack. Salt packs and the Wild Pass are optional in-app purchases.
  • Made to look real: reflections on the water, soft shadows and drifting fog, with graphics levels from Low to Ultra, and a photo mode.
  • Yours alone: it works offline, with no account, no ads and no tracking. Your life and your shop save on your device.
