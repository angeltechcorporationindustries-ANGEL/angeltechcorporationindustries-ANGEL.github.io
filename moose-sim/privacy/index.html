Privacy Policy

Effective November 23, 2026

Moose Simulator is a wildlife game for iPhone and iPad made by Angel Tech Corporation Industries L.L.C. (“we”, “us”). This policy explains what happens to your information when you play it. It is part of the Terms of Use.

The short version
  • Moose Simulator collects no data. We don’t receive anything about you or how you play.
  • No accounts, no ads, no tracking, no analytics, no purchases.
  • The game works offline and makes no internet connections of its own.
  • Your saved game and settings stay on your device.

What stays on your device

The game saves your moose’s life as you play (the day, the season, your needs, antlers, goals and journal), your graphics and sound choices, and the version and date of the Terms of Use you agreed to. All of it is stored only on your device, inside the game. We can’t see it, and it isn’t sent anywhere.

To erase your saved life, open the pause menu and tap Start a new life. Deleting the game deletes everything it saved.

Network connections

Everything Moose Simulator needs (the valley, the animals, the sounds, the code and the fonts) is inside the app, so it works in airplane mode and contacts no servers of ours or anyone else’s. The only way to leave the game is a link you choose to tap, such as this policy, the Terms or the support page. Those open in your web browser, where the website’s own privacy terms apply. Our pages are hosted by GitHub Pages; like most web hosts, GitHub may keep short technical logs, such as IP addresses, to run and secure the service. We don’t use them to identify anyone.

Apple

You get the game from Apple’s App Store, under Apple’s own privacy policy. Apple gives us only download and crash totals that don’t identify you, and only when you have allowed Apple to share them.

Children

Moose Simulator collects no personal information from anyone, so it collects none from children. Players under 18 should play with a parent’s or guardian’s permission (see the Terms of Use, Section 4).

Your rights

We hold no data about you, so there is nothing for us to access, correct or delete. If you have questions about privacy, email us and we’ll reply within 30 days.

Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we’ll update this page and the date at the top. If a future version of the game ever collected any data, we would say so here and in the release notes, and ask for your agreement in the game, before that version came out.
