How to play

  • Move: the stick at the bottom left, with your left thumb; push it all the way to go faster. The pace button switches between walk, trot and gallop.
  • Look around: drag on the right side. Pinch to zoom.
  • Act: the round buttons on the right eat or drink, bed down, call and kick. The buttons at the top open your field journal, photo mode and the pause menu.
  • With a keyboard: WASD to move, Shift to trot, Space to gallop, E to eat or drink, R to bed down, Q to call, F to kick, J for the journal. Drag to look around.

The game runs slowly or my device gets warm.

Open the pause menu and choose a lower graphics level (Low is the lightest). Auto picks a level for your device when the game starts.

Where is my saved game?

On your device, inside the game: it saves every 15 seconds of play and when you leave the app. Continue on the title screen picks up where you left off. Deleting the game deletes the save.

There is no sound.

Check that Sound is On in the pause menu, that your device isn’t in silent mode, and that the volume is up.

Why does it ask me to agree to the Terms?

The Terms of Use are the game’s license agreement. The game asks once, and again only if the Terms change. The pause menu shows the version and the date you agreed.

How do I get salt?

By playing: every journal goal, every day your moose lives and winning the rut earn salt, and there is a gift each day you play. In the iPhone and iPad app you can also buy salt packs or the Wild Pass (every coat and antler finish) in the Salt Lick shop: on the title screen, or Shop in the pause menu.

I bought something and it didn’t arrive.

Keep the game open for a moment: purchases arrive on their own, and anything paid for but not yet delivered is offered again the next time you open the game. The Wild Pass comes back on any device with Restore purchases in the shop. Salt packs are used up as you spend them, so they can’t be restored after the game is deleted. For a refund, use reportaproblem.apple.com.

Are real moose like this?

The game’s moose eat, drink, swim and rest much as real moose do, and every journal fact is meant to be true, but it is a game. Real moose are big and can be dangerous, especially cows with calves and bulls in the autumn: enjoy them from a distance.
