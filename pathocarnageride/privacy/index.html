Privacy Policy

Effective November 20, 2026

Pathocarnageride is a game made by Angel Tech Corporation Industries L.L.C. (“we”, “us”). This policy explains what the app does with your information. In short, it doesn’t collect any.

The short version
  • No accounts, no ads, no tracking, no analytics, no leaderboards.
  • The app doesn’t collect personal information. Nothing you do in the game is sent to us or anyone else.
  • Your progress and settings stay on your device.

Data that stays on your device

Your scrap, garage upgrades and paint, the sector and place you reached, your best distance, your total kills and your settings (controls, tilt steering, graphics quality and the frame-rate display) are saved on your device inside the app. We can’t see them. Deleting the app deletes them.

Motion sensors

If you switch on tilt steering, the app reads your device’s orientation to steer the bike. This happens only on your device while you play. The readings aren’t stored or sent anywhere.

No accounts or online features

Everything the game needs, including its 3D engine, is built into the app, so it plays offline. There are no accounts, servers, leaderboards, ads or analytics, so the app has no personal information to send.

Purchases

The app has no in-app purchases. If you buy the app on the App Store, Apple processes the payment, and we never receive your payment details or your Apple Account information.

Children

We don’t knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children. The app collects no personal information at all.

Your choices and rights

Because we don’t collect your data, we hold nothing about you to access, correct or delete. To remove your game data, delete the app. Depending on where you live (for example the EU, the UK or California), you may have privacy rights under local law. If you have a question, email us and we’ll reply within 30 days.

Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we’ll update this page and the date at the top. If a change affects what the app collects, we’ll also say so in the app’s release notes.
