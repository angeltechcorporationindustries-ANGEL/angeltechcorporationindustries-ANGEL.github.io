Questions people ask

How do I play?

Hold your iPhone or iPad sideways and tap START ENGINE. Steer with the thumb stick on the left. Push it left or right to move across the road, push it up to speed up and pull it back to brake. Ride around the infected or shoot your way through, and survive the boss at the end of each sector.

How do I shoot and switch guns?

Tap FIRE to shoot. Hold it for guns that keep firing, such as the minigun. The gun button shows your current gun and its ammo: tap it to choose another of the six guns. Each gun has its own ammo, and the olive-green crates on the road refill it. To fire whenever an infected is in range, turn on Settings › Auto-fire.

What do the other buttons do?

GRENADE throws a grenade. ICE throws an ice bomb that freezes the infected nearby; frozen ones shatter when you hit them. ROCKET fires a homing rocket from the shoulder launcher. BOOST gives the bike a burst of speed. Blue crates slow time for a few seconds so you can weave through.

Can I steer by tilting my device?

Yes. Tap ENABLE TILT STEERING on the main menu, or switch on Settings › Tilt steering. You can also change the steering sensitivity and the size of the thumb stick in Settings.

The game is slow or jerky

Open Settings (the gear) and set Quality to MED or LOW. AUTO picks a level for your device and steps down by itself if the game struggles. To check, switch on Settings › Show frame rate.

How do upgrades work?

Collect scrap as you ride, then open BIKE GARAGE from the main menu to upgrade the engine, armor and handling or to buy new paint.

Can I choose where to ride?

Tap SCENARIO on the main menu to pick the highway, the woods, the desert or the city. During a ride, a new place begins with each sector.

Does it work offline?

Yes. Everything the game needs is built into the app.

Will I lose my progress if I delete the app?

Yes. Your progress and settings are stored only on your device, so deleting the app deletes them.

How do I get a refund?

Apple handles refunds for App Store purchases. Visit reportaproblem.apple.com.
